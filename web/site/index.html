How it is made

  • Everything you see is drawn by Rust compiled to WebAssembly, straight into WebGL2: no game engine and no JavaScript 3D library (234 KB of WebAssembly, about 92 KB gzipped).
  • The pack tears along a random line, curls and drops away; the cards rise out of the slit, deal into a fan and turn face up. Each card has real thickness, a clear coat that catches studio light as it tilts, and a character floating above the art.
  • The characters were modelled and rendered with Rust + Bevy, then matted into layers. The voxel pets were ported cell for cell from their original JavaScript, checked by a fingerprint test.
  • It began as the TokenPet card pack and pets for EchoBird, made with the Playmand skill. Source: web/ in the repository.
