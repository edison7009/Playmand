The common habit. Each chosen row draws its own accent ring; with several chosen, the column of rings is the loudest thing on the page.

For rows that already carry a colour. The chosen row is a clearly stronger, even tint, and has no outline.

For neutral rows and pills. A soft light from the top edge and a slight lift; on light themes, also a step darker.
